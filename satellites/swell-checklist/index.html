Put forecast numbers in context

Height, period and direction describe parts of a forecast. Bathymetry, wind, tide and exposure affect what arrives and breaks at a beach. No fixed combination guarantees suitable conditions. Read the wave-period definition and ask a local professional to help interpret the day.

ASF Previsão is a separate forecasting resource; this checklist fetches no live conditions.

Further learning and limitations

Review the RNLI surfing checklist and National Weather Service rip-current guidance. Flag conventions and emergency arrangements vary by place. In an emergency, alert lifeguards and local emergency services; do not attempt an untrained water rescue.
