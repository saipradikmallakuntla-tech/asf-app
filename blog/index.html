Three foundations for a thoughtful first session

Rip currents and ocean safety

Understand the limits of visual warning signs, plan with lifeguards, and learn why conserving energy and calling for help matter.

Surf etiquette and sharing the lineup

Work through priority, positioning, communication and mistakes without treating any rule as permission to risk a collision.

Choosing a beginner surfboard

Compare stability, dimensions, construction, rental options and equipment condition before committing to a first board.
